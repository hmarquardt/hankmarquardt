What this is

This site is the long version of a résumé: the things I've actually shipped, the questions behind them, and what I learned building them. Some of it is commercial work. Most of it started as an experiment in my own time, and I try to be clear about which is which.

I like tools that explain themselves, software that can be handed over, and writing that admits what it doesn't know. When a project can't support a claim, I'd rather publish the uncertainty than a confident-looking number.
